<span style="font-size:18px; font-weight:bold;">Perks</span>
*Study.* By spending at least 4 hours in a day reading in the library, you get the choice between the skills Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature or Religion. You get proficiency or expertise if you are already proficient in the chosen skill. This effect lasts until you finish your second long rest and this can be done once per long rest.
For every additional 1 hours spent in the library until your next long rest you get a +1 to the chosen skill(s) or for every additional 2 hours spent in the library until your next long rest you get to choose an additional skill.

*Basic Creature Section* while holding the *Book of Information* you can take a magical action and expend 1 charge. When you do you gain information on target creature and the book creates and entry filling out a page with the information received if no information is gained the book gains one charge and it only expends a charge if the target is a creature and has previously not recorded any information on it

<span style="font-size:18px; font-weight:bold;">Actions</span>
*Create Book of Information.* You pay N10,000 and create a Magical item called *Book of Information*. The book is bound to you and if destroyed can be recreated with this action retaining all previously obtained information and it has 2 charge and can't have more than 2 charge.

*Recharge Magical Book.* You give your hireling a *Book of Information* and order your hireling to recharge it. The magical item regains 2 charge.
