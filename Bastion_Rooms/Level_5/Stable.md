# Stable
**Initial Size** 8
**Max Size** 32
**Cost to Build** 1000 GP
**Time to Build** 
**Cost to Expand** 50
**Requirements** 2 2x2 areas
**Exclusivity Group** None
**Expansion Slots** 
**Minimum Level** 
**Hirelings** 2

## Perks
*Animal Handling.* The stable can hold and care for large and smaller beasts. The number of creatures it can hold is based on number the built squares. Each large creature takes 4, medium and small take 1, and tiny takes 1/4. The beasts held in the stable are cared for and well fed.

## Actions
*Acquire Animals.* The stable and acquire a number of beasts (CR 1/8th or less) equal to your proficiency bonus that can be found in the area. The focus will generally be on beasts of burden or riding, like donkeys and horses.

### Stable Expansion
**Square Cost** 4
**Cost to Build** 10,000 GP
**Time to Build** 
**Requirements** 32 built Squares.
**Exclusive With** None
**Minimum Level** 11
**Hirelings Added** 

#### Perks
*Extra Space.* The space in the Stable can be expanded beyond the max size.
The max size grows to 48 and all decorational squares can be upgraded to also house creatures (takes no time). Each extra square and upgrade costs 500 GP.

#### Action


### Space Compression
**Square Cost** 12
**Cost to Build** 50,000 GP
**Time to Build** 
**Requirements** 48 built squares, Stable Expansion
**Exclusive With** None
**Minimum Level** 15
**Hirelings Added** 

#### Perks
*Compressed Space*. The space in the Stable is compressed, allowing every square to count as 2/3/4 squares in terms of housing creatures at the levels 15/17/19.
Additionally, the max squares increases to 64, with each square costing 1000 GP.
