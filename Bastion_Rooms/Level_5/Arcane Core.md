<span style="font-size:18px; font-weight:bold;">Description</span>
The arcane core is special, in that it is the power center of all bastions. It is what brings magic to all of the wondrous features of the bastion. 

This feature is only relevant if you use the “Arcane Forge” rules extension by Mystic Arts. 


<span style="font-size:22px; font-weight:bold;">Effects</span>
<span style="font-size:18px; font-weight:bold;">Perks</span>
*Lesser Arcane Core*. The Lesser Arcane Core holds just enough energy to power basic rooms around the Bastion and to assist in creating #Common results.

*Core Restriction.* This room can never be larger than a 6x6 square area.
