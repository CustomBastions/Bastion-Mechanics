# Specific Rules
Hirelings come in a few different tiers depending on how skilled they have to be.

Higher tiered hirelings require a higher wage and better living conditions if they are housed. 

If a Hireling is housed, their wage can be halved. Though in return each room has an expense associated with it depending on the tier.
## Tier 1
Tier 1 Hirelings are essentially commoners, so anything that’s to be expected for commoners to handle should be manageable by these hirelings. 

They expect a basic 2x3 square room with a bed, a small chest for personal items, and a closet.
For pay they expect N250-500 (before tax) per bastion turn (this assumes a week long turn). The specific price is decided by the job.
