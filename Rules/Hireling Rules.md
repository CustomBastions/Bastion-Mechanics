# Specific Rules
Hirelings come in a few different tiers depending on how skilled they have to be.

Higher tiered hirelings require a higher wage and better living conditions if they are housed. 

If a Hireling is housed, their wage can be halved. Though in return each room has an expense associated with it depending on the tier.

The expected pay will be given as a range and assumes a bastion turn length of one week. In case taxes are important, then the pay is before tax. The specific pay is specified when a feature says that a hireling is needed.

## Tier 1
Tier 1 Hirelings are essentially commoners, so anything that’s to be expected for commoners to handle should be manageable by these hirelings. They mostly do manual labor, but the more skilled ones may have a single proficiency.

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 2x3
**Furniture**
- A simple bed
- A small chest for personal items
- A closet
**Room cost per turn** ɴ400
**Wage** ɴ1,000-2,000

## Tier 2
Tier 2 hirelings are skilled professionals compared to the general population. Though they are still not the biggest specialists. They typically have multiple proficiencies, access to a cantrip or two, or basic combat techniques.

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 3x4
**Furniture**
- 
**Room cost per turn** ɴ1,000
**Wage** ɴ2,000-8,000

## Tier 3
Tier 3 hirelings are people who have started to touch upon the path to mastery. They have multiple proficiencies, an expertise, a few level 1 or 2 spells available, or the combat power of newer adventurers. 

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 2x(3x4), 2x3
**Furniture**
- 
**Room cost per turn** ɴ4,000
**Wage** ɴ8,000-30,000

## Tier 4
Tier 4 hirelings are strong enough to rule small towns if they wanted, as they can have access to level 3-4 spells or the ability to withstand hit after hit. They are often the leaders of guild branches, knights, or the main force of an arcane court.
They have the potential to craft #Common  magic items.

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 2x(4x5), 3x4
**Furniture**
- 
**Room cost per turn** ɴ15,000
**Wage** ɴ30,000-80,000

## Tier 5
Tier 5 hirelings are generally the powerhouses of most nations when grouped together, as their access to level 5-6 spells and incredible physical prowess pose serious threat to most foes. They are often guild leaders, royal mages, royal guard captains, or something of similar prestige.
They have the potential to craft #Uncommon  magic items.

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 2x(4x6), 3x5, 2x(2x4)
**Furniture**
- 
**Room cost per turn** ɴ40,000
**Wage** ɴ80,000-400,000

## Tier 6
Tier 6 hirelings can have access to level 7 spells and near unparalleled physical prowess. Most nations have only a handful of people at this level, though larger ones tend to have a fair few.
They have the potential to craft #Rare  magic items.

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 2x(5x6), 3x(4x5), 2x(2x3), 1x2
**Furniture**
- 
**Room cost per turn** ɴ200,000
**Wage** ɴ400,000-1,000,000

## Tier 7
Tier 7 hirelings are so close to the peak that many reach their end trying to make it the last step. In return, they are people who have the power to forcefully rule smaller nations, if they so desired. They have access to eighth level magic.
They have the potential to craft #VeryRare  magic items.

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 2x(5x7), 3x(4x5), 2x(3x4), 1x2
**Furniture**
- 
**Room cost per turn** ɴ500,000
**Wage** ɴ1,000,000-2,000,000

## Tier 8
Tier 8 hirelings are absolute masters of their specialty and even finding any to hire is only possible by the most accomplished of people. The spellcasters have access to ninth level magic and the martials would make even dragon fear a direct confrontation. Even great nations count themselves lucky to have more than a few people of this status.
They have the potential to craft #Legendary magic items.

<span style="font-size: 20px; font-weight:bold;">Expectations</span>
**Room size** 8x10, 2x(5x7), 3x(4x5), 2x(3x4), 2x(1x2)
**Furniture**
- 
**Room cost per turn** ɴ1,000,000
**Wage** ɴ2,000,000-8,000,000
